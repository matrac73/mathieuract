The biggest day of our trip so far.

Off at 8 am with the climb of the Stelvio: 2,000 m of elevation in one go. It takes us 3h15 to reach the top, with no regrets. A magnificent road, with its 48 hairpins and glaciers in the background. After that, everything else will taste a little bland 😵‍💫

We then string together 4 passes of 200 to 1,000 m of climbing each, which takes us past the symbolic mark of 4,000 m of elevation in a single day.

Arriving in St. Moritz was much appreciated. After some shopping in a supermarket at the station (the only one still open), we pitch camp and have dinner above the lake before a well-earned sleep!

Hats off to Et., who had never climbed more than 1,500 m in a day before this one!
