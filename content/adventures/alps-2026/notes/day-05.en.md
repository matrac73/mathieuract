A long day.

Waking up beside the cycle path, on the Italian-Austrian border, with a view of a glacier on the opposite slope.

Then batteries recharged, both muscular and electric, in Vipiteno, on Italian cappuccinos (FYI, the Italians of this region speak German).

We have to say goodbye to Ma. and Ya., who carry on east towards Croatia. Bye bye and good luck.

Et. and I head for the Passo di Monte Giovo, our climb of the day (1,000 m of elevation): a magnificent pass, but a bit steep for my little frog's thighs.

We reach the top without trouble. Then an 18 km descent with a stunning view over the southern Italian Alps (Au., you would have loved it).

Once lunch is swallowed and the click click click pshitt pshitt pshitt bzz bzz bzz of Et.'s bike are fixed, we take the road to the Stelvio. I pictured 60 flat kilometres (rest): it turned into 60 km of false flat uphill (1,000 m of climbing in total! As much as the Giovo).

We arrive wrecked at the foot of the Stelvio, but happy to be there.

We have dinner in a basic pizzeria, BUT one with a shower 🤯 We ask politely, in rough Italo-Germano-glish, whether we may use it. The waitress has to consult her superiors… She comes back and says: "they first said no, but then yes". We rush to grab our things and take our first hot shower in 4 days: aaaamazing.

We do a little laundry at the village fountain before going to bed in the stands of the town stadium, hidden between the rows — I am writing to you from here.

More tomorrow.
