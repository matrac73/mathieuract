Last day of riding for the team. Waking up at Lou's after the best night of the trip (a sleep score of 83/100). It feels like my body has got used to the conditions.

Only 3 climbs of 500 m each are left before we get home.

We leave at 9 am for the "Swiss" Col de la Forclaz. I say "Swiss" because there are three "Col de la Forclaz" within 100 km: above Lake Annecy, above Queige, and in Switzerland. That is because "Forclaz" simply means "pass" in the Savoyard dialect.

After a quick coffee at the top, we ride down to the French border! And we go straight into the Col des Montets, about 8 km at 4%, so fairly gentle, which lets me test the legs at the end of the trip — and they respond well! The sharpening worked.

We ride through Chamonix…
