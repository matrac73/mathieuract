Waking up in the gentle town of Feldkirch.

A bit of history: a Roman town once, then a Habsburg one; today this pretty place is known as the westernmost town in Austria.

Enough history, back to the bike. The day starts with a long climb (60 km), gentle at first, then steep, then very steep in the hairpins.

We are more than happy to reach the top and stop at a restaurant.

Then comes a long, gentle descent through gorgeous Austrian scenery: rivers, cycle paths, fir trees, greenery, mountains… Paradise!

We narrowly escape the rain by sheltering in the hall of a shopping centre until it passes — spoiler: we will spend the night in that area…

Camp set up in pouring rain.
