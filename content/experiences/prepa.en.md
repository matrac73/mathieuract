Two years of preparatory classes in the PTSI then PT track (physics, technology and engineering sciences) at Lycée La Martinière Monplaisir, in Lyon. It is the track where engineering sciences weigh as much as mathematics and physics, with oral exams at the blackboard every week and the Banque PT competitive exams at the end.

## First year: PTSI

### Mathematics

Everything is rebuilt from the foundations, with proofs.

- Analysis: sequences, limits, differentiation, integration, differential equations, Taylor expansions
- Algebra: complex numbers, polynomials, vector spaces, matrices and determinants
- Probability on finite sets, plane and space geometry

### Physics and chemistry

Learning to put a phenomenon into equations, then to check the result against its order of magnitude.

- Signals and oscillators, electrical circuits, geometrical optics
- Point and rigid-body mechanics, thermodynamics, induction
- Chemistry: structure of matter, equilibria in solution, redox reactions

### Engineering sciences

The subject that sets the track apart: understanding how a real system is designed, controlled and manufactured.

- Functional and structural analysis of a system
- Kinematics and statics of mechanisms
- Control systems: modelling, time and frequency response
- Design and manufacturing: technical drawing, CAD, workshop practice

### Computer science

- Python programming and algorithms
- Numerical simulation and databases

### French-philosophy and English

- One theme and three books a year, with essay and summary
- Written and spoken English

## Second year: PT

### Mathematics

- Reduction of endomorphisms, Euclidean spaces
- Numerical and power series, improper and parameter-dependent integrals
- Linear differential equations, functions of several variables, curves and surfaces
- Probability: discrete random variables

### Physics and chemistry

- Electromagnetism: Maxwell's equations and waves
- Thermodynamics of machines and flows, heat diffusion, fluid mechanics
- Electronics: filtering, amplifiers, power conversion
- Wave optics: interference
- Chemistry: thermochemistry, electrochemistry and corrosion

### Engineering sciences

- Dynamics and energetics of rigid bodies
- Control systems: performance, stability, correction
- Strength of materials and beam theory
- Mechanical design and manufacturing processes

### TIPE

A supervised personal research project: a chosen subject, an experimental or modelling approach, then an oral presentation in front of a jury.
