At Adagia Partners, a private equity fund based in Paris, I lead Tech & AI ("TechOps") across two scopes: the portfolio companies (Portco) and the management company (Manco). Below, by company and then by function, are the subjects I have worked on.

## Portco

### Schwind

German manufacturer of laser systems for refractive eye surgery.

- Consolidation and cleaning of the installed base (machines, clinics, contacts) from CRM and ERP data.
- AI market-intelligence agents: analysis of clinic websites to identify the equipment in place.
- CRM enrichment with this data and a world map of the installed base for the sales teams.
- Installed-base analyses for management, in support of marketing campaigns.
- Daily usage-tracking email for the dashboard.

### Insperial

French group providing high-performance technical adhesive solutions.

- Mapping of companies in the sector (A-Zoom): extraction, AI classification and Power BI dashboard.
- News-monitoring tool for the sales force ("augmented salesforce").
- Training of the executive committee on generative AI and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- Follow-up of the group CRM rollout and the financial data workstream.
- Advice on automating ESG reporting and the filing of technical drawings.

### Minlay

European maker and distributor of dental prostheses.

- Follow-up of the group's business intelligence programme.
- Machine-learning prospecting pipeline built on public data, connected to the data warehouse and the CRM.
- Mapping of companies in the sector across Europe.
- Support for the selection and then the rollout of the sales CRM.
- "AI use cases" workshop with the sales teams and the technical leadership.

### Technoflex

French manufacturer of flexible bags for injectable drugs, the world leader in its market.

- Mapping of companies in the sector through extraction and AI classification of databases.
- Analysis of public customs data for market knowledge.
- 360° tech, data and AI audit, and specification of a management data warehouse.
- AI use-case approach: awareness sessions, business interviews, prioritisation, then an on-site sprint week.
- Three use cases delivered (R&D, HR, supply chain), then handover to and training of the IT team.

### AGN

German architecture and engineering group.

- Mapping of companies in the sector (A-Zoom) and Power BI dashboard handed over to management.
- Scoping of the data warehouse and management cockpit: current state, target, choice of an integrator.
- Identification and prioritisation of AI use cases with the leadership team.
- PMO role on tech priorities: CRM, sales pipeline, BI.

### PatrimOne

Independent insurance broker specialised in private clients.

- 360° tech, data and AI audit: risk mapping and value-creation levers.
- Preparation of the value-creation plan kick-off with management (interviews, workshops).
- Power BI map of companies in the sector, produced by an automated generator.
- Scoping of two data projects: single client repository and prospect data.

## Manco

### Origination

- A-Scout: company extraction from Gain.pro, filtering and AI classification against a custom taxonomy.
- Sector reviews: segmentation into sub-sectors and classification of tens of thousands of companies.
- A-Tlas: generator of sector reports (market, players, trends) run by a team of AI agents.
- A-Zoom: focused mappings with web enrichment, delivered as a dashboard.
- A-pp: internal application bringing these tools together, deployed on Azure with CI/CD and usage tracking.

### Investment team

- 360° tech, data and AI audit methodology, used in due diligence and after acquisition.
- Assessment grid for the risk of AI disruption of a target company.
- Contribution to the grid used to analyse investment cases.
- Repository of AI use cases and a method to transpose them to a new portfolio company.
- Claude skill for NDA review, built with the team.

### Investor relations

- Investor CRM in Notion: data model, migration of records, usage tracking.
- Answers to investors' due diligence questionnaires on the tech and AI sections.
- Tech & AI presentations for investors.
- Demo videos of portfolio use cases, in French and English, with AI voice-over.

### Network and recruiting

- A-Link: search for executives and experts, then enrichment of contact details.
- Base of experts and senior advisors structured by sector and function in the Notion CRM.
- Consolidation of the team's contact base.
- Recruitment and supervision of tech interns, scoping of a tech analyst position.

### Transversal
