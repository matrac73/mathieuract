After the engineering degree, ESSEC's Grande École programme (Master in Management), from 2022 to 2024: a first year on the Singapore campus, in the Business Management in Asia track, then a second year in Cergy, as an apprentice at Onepoint.

## Singapore, 2022-2023

The year of management fundamentals, taught in English on the Asia-Pacific campus.

### Courses

- Accounting: reading and building a company's financial statements (an exam of nearly 3 hours in December 2022)
- Corporate finance (an exam of nearly 3 hours in December 2022)
- Marketing, with group work
- Strategy and management
- Artificial intelligence for business, as an online course from May 2023
- Chinese, up to HSK 2

### Around the courses

- Career workshops: CV and cover letter, interviews, "pyramid thinking" to structure an answer
- Group project with a visit to a Singaporean company
- Talks by executives, career fair and the school's mentoring programme
- Consulting case-interview practice, in pairs, in spring 2023

## Cergy, 2023-2024

The second year is a work-study year: classes in Cergy, the rest of the time at Onepoint.

- Diversity and inclusion: an online path of three modules, with peer assessment
- Carbon & AI: a three-day seminar on the carbon footprint of artificial intelligence
- Negotiation, through simulations
- Chinese: oral presentation about Singapore
- Master's thesis, handed in in March 2024
